# Project Card

## Project Topic
Descriptive analysis of Body Mass Index (BMI) and Systolic Blood Pressure in adults aged 20+.

## Main Statistical Question
Is there a significant difference in mean BMI across different age groups and sexes in adults aged 20 and older, and does this difference hold when accounting for NHANES sampling weights?

## Observation Unit
Each individual participating in the NHANES survey.

## Analysis Unit
Each individual (one independent observation per person).

## Main Variables
- BMI: Continuous quantitative (`BMXBMI`)
- Systolic BP: Continuous quantitative (`BPXOSY1`)
- Age: Quantitative/Ordinal (`RIDAGEYR`)
- Sex: Nominal binary (`RIAGENDR`)
- Weight: Sampling weight (`WTMEC2YR`)

## Type of Question
Descriptive and comparative.
